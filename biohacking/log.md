# Biohacking Log

Entries are made whenever something meaningful changes or is observed; this is not a daily or weekly journal.

## Entry format

```markdown
### YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm

**Changed:** What was deliberately changed.

**Observed:** What happened, without asserting a cause.

**Context:** Optional details that may affect interpretation.
```

## Entries

### 2026-09-10

**Changed:** No deliberate intervention; this entry establishes the initial baseline.

**Observed:** Severe afternoon lethargy and brain fog around 14:00–16:00. Abdominal fat accumulation and an increase in waist size from approximately 74 cm to 78 cm were observed over the preceding year. Afternoon sugar cravings were common.

**Context:** Heavy cognitive workload. At approximately 09:00, sweetened coffee containing condensed milk and sweetened fresh milk was consumed after climbing 5–9 floors on an empty stomach. Lunch at approximately 12:00 was rice-heavy, with a 300 m walk each way and a 15–30 minute nap around 12:50. Biscuits or candies were commonly used during afternoon energy dips. Bodyweight exercise was performed before a fast dinner at approximately 21:00 on 3–5 days per week. Fruit intake was very low.

### 2026-09-14

**Changed:** Removed added sugar from morning coffee. Partially adopted vegetables or soup before protein and carbohydrates at lunch. Added fruit immediately after lunch. Continued the approximately 600 m round-trip walk and maintained at least 1.5 L of water intake. Some days were caffeine-free.

**Observed:** Mental clarity and concentration were slightly better during morning and early-afternoon work. Mild afternoon fatigue remained. No severe lethargy was noticed on caffeine-free days. Fruit after lunch appeared more satiating and was associated with steadier perceived energy than high-sugar pantry snacks.

**Context:** Several factors changed together, including added sugar, caffeine exposure on some days, meal sequencing, fruit intake, walking, and snack choice. Their individual effects cannot yet be separated.
